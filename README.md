# DeepThought-AI-Business-Analyst-Assignment-Ecommerce-
Sales Account Management Simulation
Project Overview

This project is a simulated account management assignment focused on analyzing client accounts, preparing status updates, creating MD discussion points, and reflecting on account management strategies.

The assignment demonstrates:

Account analysis and performance review
Client communication planning
Strategic growth recommendations
Reflection on account management decision-making
Tasks Completed
Task 1: Account Analysis

Reviewed assigned client accounts and identified:

Current account performance
Key trends and patterns
Challenges affecting account growth
Opportunities for improvement
Task 2: Status Update Preparation

Prepared structured updates for the team lead covering:

Account health
Performance concerns
Immediate actions required
Priority follow-ups
Task 3: Strategic Reflection & MD Talking Points

Created:

Talking points for Managing Director discussion
Growth recommendations based on account insights
Reflection note on cross-account patterns
Process improvement suggestions
Key Skills Demonstrated
Strategic account analysis
Business communication
Stakeholder reporting
Problem-solving
Growth opportunity identification
Critical reflection


Through this project, I developed a stronger understanding of:

How account managers evaluate business performance
The difference between operational reporting and strategic discussion
How to identify growth opportunities using account-level insights
The importance of clear stakeholder communication
